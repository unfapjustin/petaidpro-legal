❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and select Pet First Aid CPR App to cancel. You can cancel anytime before the renewal date.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
The Annual plan includes a 7-day free trial. You won't be charged until the trial ends. Cancel before the 7 days are up and you won't be billed anything.
How do I restore my purchases on a new device?
Open the app and tap Restore Purchases on the paywall screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used to subscribe.
Is the first aid information accurate?
All content is for educational purposes only and based on veterinary training material. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for professional medical advice in emergencies.
What animals does the app cover?
Dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, rodents, fish, and reptiles.
I was charged but the app shows free features only.
Please try tapping Restore Purchases on the paywall screen. If the issue persists, email us at cprwestcovina@gmail.com.
